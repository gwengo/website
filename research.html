IoBT Catalogue

A field guide to living things in a near future shaped by the Internet of Biological Things.

The catalogue imagines a planetary network of living bacterial sensors in bodies, infrastructure, and ecosystems. It asks how living things come to be classified, valued, and traded.

I make the collages and write the text. Devlyn Kornegay illustrates and contributes visual design. The project draws on Black ecological thought and was my contribution to Black Ecologies.

SubWave

Linocut wildlife and a projection that moves with the subway.

Hand-pulled linocut prints of East River estuary wildlife, paired with a TouchDesigner projection triggered by subway vibrations, plus a zine. Research draws on German Expressionist printmaking, slow-craft discourse, and urban ecology. Projected outdoors in DUMBO in May 2026 through NYU’s collaboration with the DUMBO Improvement District.

Looking back: The trigger read subway noise as continuous, so the projection almost never returned to rest. The broken sensor came to feel like the truest part of the piece.

open-collage

Transparent PNGs from my relief prints, free to take apart and reuse.

A GitHub repository organized by what an image does (texture, edge, mark, ground). An Are.na channel connects the collection to related visual thinking.

generative art & writing · 2025 to 2026

[image: a zine spread or generator output]

Authorship in Dissolution

A zine and poem generator about who writes when many agents shape a text.

A 15-page interactive HTML/PDF zine with a working Mary Oliver–style poem generator built from semantic fields, phonetic operators, and a concreteness arc. The argument draws on Nake, LeWitt, Barthes, Chomsky, and Baudrillard.

Looking back: The generator wrote lines more tender than I expected from a machine. That unsettled me more than the theory did.
